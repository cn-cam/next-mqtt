# Trademark and naming policy

NextMQTT is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, sponsored by, or certified by any commercial MQTT broker vendor.

## Reserved project marks

The names **NextMQTT** and **next-mqtt**, and the project logo, identify this
project. You may use them to refer to the unmodified project (for example
"built with NextMQTT" or "NextMQTT 0.1.0"). You may not use them in a way that
suggests your fork, product or service is the official project, or that the
project endorses it.

## Third-party marks

This repository deliberately contains no third-party broker product names,
logos, slogans, configuration formats or screenshots. Contributors must not
add them. See `AGENTS.md` section 1 for the mechanically enforced rule.

"MQTT" is a specification maintained by OASIS. References to MQTT in this
project describe protocol compliance with the published OASIS standard and do
not imply OASIS endorsement.

## Forks and redistributions

If you redistribute NextMQTT, you are responsible for:

1. keeping the Apache-2.0 licence and `NOTICE` file;
2. not implying official status;
3. not adding third-party broker branding to the redistributed work.

## Reporting a concern

Open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers listed in
`SECURITY.md` if you believe a mark is used incorrectly in this repository.
We remove or rename offending content promptly.
